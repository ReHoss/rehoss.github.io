# Checklist for new researchers

A practical checklist for onboarding a new lab member (intern, PhD student,
postdoc, visitor). Items were consolidated after several real arrivals; not all
apply to every status.

## Administrative & HR (settle before, or on, the first day)

- Employment contract / internship convention **signed by all parties** before
  the first working day — without it the person is not covered.
- Security clearance (*avis de sécurité*), required for non-EU nationals —
  request it early: it can delay the start by several days to weeks and gates
  the badge request.
- Bank details (RIB) provided to the management team — no salary / gratification
  is paid without it.
- Public-transport subscription reimbursement (e.g. Navigo, 50 %).
- Leave and telework policy agreed explicitly (the amount of telework is often
  asked for in the end-of-stay evaluation).
- End of contract: attestation / certificate requested; evaluation form and
  report where applicable.

## Mandatory trainings & compliance

- New-arrivals occupational-risk (safety) training.
- Confidentiality & information-asset charter.
- AI-systems acceptable-use charter.
- Occupational-health visit (*médecine du travail*).

## General & team

- Welcome tour and introductions, including the **management / HR team** (the
  people to contact for everything below).
- Workspace setup: computer, monitor, ethernet, keyboard, docking station,
  office key.
- Badge, and the **building-access hours and badge zones** it actually opens
  (own wing and office, not just the main entrance).
- Printers access.
- Canteen / lunch-account activation.
- Login credentials and institutional email (mail client on the workstation
  **and** on the phone).
- Team & seminar mailing lists.
- Meeting & appointment dates; a recurring supervision slot.

## Health & safety

- Evacuation routes, assembly point, floor wardens and first-aiders.

## Tools

- Intranet / wiki.
- Internal GitLab / code-repository access.
- Instant-messaging application.
- LLM / AI assistants.
- HPC / compute-cluster access: account, SSH, and VPN for remote access.
- Data storage & backup (shared storage, quotas).

## Campus life

- Sports facilities.
- Commute: public transport + shuttle, or bike.
